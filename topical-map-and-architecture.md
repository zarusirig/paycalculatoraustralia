### Architecture Decision: Flat Structure

**Rationale**: All pages are 1 click from the homepage. This is intentional because:
1. **Crawl depth**: Every page is within 1 click — maximum crawl priority
2. **Calculator site pattern**: Users expect direct access, not nested navigation
3. **30-page site**: Small enough that flat architecture is optimal
4. **No folder nesting**: `/calculators/income-tax/` adds nothing — the slug is self-descriptive
5. **Breadcrumb hierarchy**: Still established via BreadcrumbList schema (Home > [Page])
