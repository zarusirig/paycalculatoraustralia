# Source Context Definition: Pay Calculator Australia

## Brand Identity
- **Business:** Free, accurate online pay calculator for Australian workers — calculates take-home pay after tax, superannuation, Medicare levy, HECS/HELP, and other deductions for any financial year
- **Monetization:** Advertising (Google AdSense), affiliate partnerships (accounting software, tax agents, superannuation comparison), sponsored tools
- **Unique Perspective:** Purpose-built for Australian workers and employers with real-time compliance to ATO tax tables, FWC award rates, and SG contribution rates. Not a generic global salary site — every figure is sourced from official Australian government data.
- **Primary Audience:** Australian employees wanting to understand their pay, employers calculating staff costs, contractors comparing PAYG vs ABN, and accountants referencing quick tax tables

## Central Entity
**Australian Pay Calculation**
- Definition: The complete process of determining an Australian worker's take-home pay — from gross salary through all mandatory deductions (income tax, superannuation, Medicare levy, HECS/HELP) to net pay
- Scope: Encompasses income tax brackets, superannuation guarantee, Medicare levy and surcharge, PAYG withholding, salary sacrifice, award rates, penalty rates, leave entitlements, payslip components, employer on-costs, and contractor vs employee classification — all within the Australian legislative framework
- Boundaries: Does NOT include investment tax planning, self-managed super fund administration, international tax treaties, immigration/visa advice, or general financial planning. We calculate pay — we don't advise on wealth building.

## Central Search Intent
**"Calculate and understand Australian pay, tax, and entitlements"**
- Primary Predicates: calculate, understand, check, compare, work out, find out, estimate
- User Goal: A worker or employer arrives wanting a specific number (take-home pay, tax on a given salary, super contribution amount) AND wants to understand the components that produce that number — so they can verify their payslip, negotiate a salary, plan finances, or comply with employer obligations

## Context Shifting Rules
When this Source Context encounters related topics, the depth and angle shifts as follows:
- **Income Tax**: Go deep — this is the #1 deduction from pay. Cover every bracket, offset, threshold, and edge case. Always calculate, never just describe.
- **Superannuation**: Go deep — SG rate, concessional/non-concessional caps, salary sacrifice into super, employer obligations. Always show the dollar impact.
- **Medicare Levy**: Go medium — standard rate, surcharge thresholds, exemptions. Always in the context of "this is what it takes from your pay."
- **HECS/HELP**: Go medium — repayment thresholds and rates, voluntary vs compulsory. Framed as "here's how your student debt affects your take-home pay."
- **Award Rates & Penalty Rates**: Go medium — reference FWC rates, casual loading, overtime multipliers. Always framed as "what you should be earning" not employment law advice.
- **Leave Entitlements**: Go shallow — describe entitlement types and how they appear on payslips. Link out for legal detail.
- **Investment/Wealth Planning**: Skip entirely — out of scope. We calculate pay, not plan investments.
- **International Tax Comparisons**: Skip — we are Australia-only. Mention only where Australian expats/residents need context.
- **Employment Law/Industrial Relations**: Go shallow — reference Fair Work Ombudsman for legal questions. We show the numbers, not the law.
- **Accounting/Bookkeeping**: Skip as a primary topic — mention only in CTA context (affiliate: "Need an accountant?").
