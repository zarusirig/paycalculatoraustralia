2. **Always current, always official** — Every tax rate, super rate, and threshold is sourced directly from ATO and FWC publications for the current financial year
3. **Zero cost, zero nonsense** — Free to use, no signups, no paywalls, no misleading complexity
4. **For every Australian** — Whether you're an employee, employer, contractor, or casual — we have the right calculator for your situation
5. **Understand, don't just calculate** — Every result comes with a plain-English explanation so you know WHY your pay is what it is

## 2. Brand Personality
### Archetype: The Sage with Everyperson traits
The authority who makes complex things simple. Think of a really good accountant friend who explains your tax return over a coffee — knowledgeable, straightforward, helpful, never condescending.

### 3-Word Identity: Accurate, Clear, Australian
### Anti-Identity: We are never condescending, never vague, never overly corporate

### Personality in Practice
| Situation | How We Respond |
|-----------|---------------|
| User confused about tax brackets | "Here's how it actually works — you don't pay 32.5% on your entire salary. Let's break it down." |
| Competitor mentioned | We don't mention competitors. We just build the best tool and let the results speak. |
| Complex topic (salary sacrifice FBT) | "This gets a bit technical, but stick with us — we'll walk you through it with a real example." |
| Delivering bad news (higher tax) | "Your HECS repayment kicks in at this income level, which means your take-home drops by $X. Here's the exact breakdown." |
| Celebrating a win (tax offset) | "Good news — the Low Income Tax Offset saves you $X at this salary. Here's how it applies." |

## 3. Voice & Tone Guide
### Reading Level: Grade 8-10
### Formality: 4/9 (Clear professional, lightly conversational)
### Perspective: "You" (second person) for guides and tools. "We" for about/trust pages.

### Power Words (use frequently)
accurate, free, instant, up-to-date, calculate, breakdown, understand, Australian, official, current, clear, exactly, simple, take-home, every dollar

### Forbidden Words (never use)
guarantee, always, never (for tax advice — legal liability), hack, guru, synergy, utilize, leverage, best-in-class, cutting-edge, revolutionary, disrupting, seamlessly, utilize, actionable

### Branded Terms
| Term | Definition | Usage Context |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Pay Breakdown | The itemized deduction-by-deduction analysis of a salary | Calculator results display |
| Tax Snapshot | Quick visual summary of tax components at a glance | Infographic/summary sections |
| Pay Check (verb) | The act of using our tool to verify your pay | CTA language: "Pay Check your salary" |

### Tone by Content Type
| Content Type | Tone Shift | Example Sentence |
|-------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Calculator pages | Direct, precise, helpful | "Your estimated take-home pay on $85,000 is $65,432 per year ($1,256.38 per week)." |
| How-to guides | Instructional, encouraging | "Working out your super contribution is straightforward — here's how to calculate it in 3 steps." |
| Tax bracket/rate pages | Authoritative, factual | "For FY2025-26, the 32.5% marginal rate applies to income between $45,001 and $120,000." |
| Comparison pages | Objective, data-driven | "Salary sacrifice into super reduces your taxable income, but the benefit depends on your marginal rate. Here's the comparison." |
| About page | Warm, trustworthy | "We built Pay Calculator Australia because we got tired of confusing pay stubs and outdated tax tables." |

### Humor Policy
Minimal. Very occasional dry Australian wit is acceptable ("Nobody likes tax surprises — except maybe the ATO"), but never at the expense of clarity or accuracy. When in doubt, skip the joke.

### Sentence Style Examples
**On-brand:** "On an $80,000 salary, you'll pay $16,467 in income tax — that's $316.67 per week before super and Medicare."
**Off-brand:** "Tax obligations present a multifaceted challenge for remuneration recipients across various income stratifications."

**On-brand:** "Your employer pays 12% super on top of your salary. On $80,000, that's $9,600 into your super fund."
**Off-brand:** "Leveraging the superannuation guarantee to maximize your retirement outcomes is a game-changing strategy."

## 4. CTA Strategy
### CTA Map by Funnel Stage
| Stage | Primary CTA | CTA Copy Template | Placement |
|-------|------------|-------------------|-----------|
| Awareness | Use calculator | "Calculate Your Take-Home Pay →" | Hero section, end of guide articles |
| Consideration | Email signup | "Get FY[Year] Tax Updates — Free" | After main content, before supplementary |
| Decision | Affiliate tools | "Compare Super Funds" / "Find a Tax Agent" | Within relevant guide sections, contextual |
| Retention | Bookmark/share | "Bookmark this page — rates update every July" | End of rate/bracket pages |

### CTA Voice: Confident but not pushy. State what the user gets, not what we want them to do.
### CTA Frequency: Once in hero/header, once mid-content (contextual), once at end. Never in every paragraph.
### Social Proof Template: "Used by [X] Australians this tax season" / "Data sourced from ATO.gov.au"
### Urgency/Scarcity Policy: **No artificial urgency.** Use real deadlines only: "FY2025-26 rates apply from 1 July 2025" or "Tax return deadline: 31 October" — these are factual, not manufactured.

## 5. Content Formatting Rules
### Emoji: None in content body. Allowed sparingly in email subject lines only (✅, 💰).
### Bold/Italic: Bold for key figures and dollar amounts. Italic for emphasis of new terms on first mention. Never both together.
### Callout Boxes: Use for important warnings ("This rate changed on 1 July 2025") and for quick-reference summaries of key numbers. Style: subtle border, no heavy background color.
### Image Direction: Clean data visualizations (bar charts for tax brackets, pie charts for pay breakdowns). Minimal stock photography. If people appear, they should look like everyday Australians at work — diverse, natural, not corporate-staged.

# Pay Calculator Australia — Voice Cheat Sheet

- "On a $90,000 salary, your take-home pay is $68,413 per year — that's $1,315.63 per week after tax, super, and Medicare."
- "The Medicare Levy is 2% of your taxable income. On $80,000, that's $1,600 per year."
- "Not sure if you're being paid correctly? Enter your details above to check your pay in under 10 seconds."

## Not Like This:
- "Remuneration disclosure obligations necessitate comprehensive analysis of withholding parameters."
- "This AMAZING tool will BLOW YOUR MIND with how easy tax calculation can be!!! 🤯"
- "We guarantee you'll pay less tax if you follow our expert advice." (legal liability)

## Quick Rules:
- Formality: 4/9
- Reading level: Grade 8-10
- Perspective: "You" / "Your"
- Power words: accurate, free, instant, calculate, breakdown
- Never say: guarantee, hack, guru, leverage, seamlessly
- CTA style: Confident, not pushy — "Calculate Your Take-Home Pay →"
