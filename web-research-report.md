## 1. Verified Financial Data (FY2025-26)

### Income Tax Brackets (Australian Residents)
| Taxable Income | Tax Rate |
|---------------|----------|
| $0 – $18,200 | Nil |
| $18,201 – $45,000 | 16c per $1 over $18,200 |
| $45,001 – $135,000 | $4,288 + 30c per $1 over $45,000 |
| $135,001 – $190,000 | $31,288 + 37c per $1 over $135,000 |
| $190,001+ | $51,638 + 45c per $1 over $190,000 |

- Tax-free threshold: $18,200
- LITO effective tax-free threshold: $22,575
- Upcoming change: FY2026-27 lowest rate drops from 16% to 15%
- Source: ATO, confirmed by H&R Block, PwC, SuperGuide

### Superannuation Guarantee
- **SG Rate**: 12% (from 1 July 2025 — final increase)
- **Max contribution base**: $62,500/quarter ($250,000/year)
- **Max quarterly SG**: $7,500
- Based on Ordinary Time Earnings (OTE)
- Source: ATO, AustralianSuper, Industry Super

### Medicare Levy
- **Standard rate**: 2% of taxable income
- **Surcharge**: 1% to 1.5% for higher earners without private health insurance
- **Low-income threshold (singles)**: ~$27,222 (FY2024-25, FY2025-26 TBC)
- Source: ATO, Moneysmart

### HECS/HELP Repayment (FY2025-26 — NEW SYSTEM)
| Repayment Income | Rate |
|-----------------|------|
| $0 – $67,000 | Nil |
| $67,001 – $125,000 | 15c per $1 over $67,000 |
| $125,001 – $179,285 | $8,700 + 17c per $1 over $125,000 |
| $179,286+ | 10% of total repayment income |

- **Key change**: Now marginal (only income ABOVE threshold is repaid), not flat rate on total income
- Threshold raised from $54,435 to $67,000
- Source: ATO, Department of Education

### Low Income Tax Offset (LITO)
- Max offset: $700 (income ≤ $37,500)
- Phases out: $37,501 – $45,000 (at 5c per $1)
- Further phases: $45,001 – $66,667 (at 1.5c per $1)
- Nil at income > $66,667
- Source: ATO, SuperGuide

## 2. Competitor Landscape

### Top Competitors
| Competitor | Domain | Strength | Weakness |
|-----------|--------|----------|----------|
| **paycalculator.com.au** | Exact-match domain | Strong brand, clean calculator | Limited guide content |
| **atotaxcalculator.com.au** | Authority domain | Well-known, trusted | Focuses only on tax, limited scope |
| **australiapaycalculator.com.au** | Long-tail match | Covers FY2025-26 rates | UX dated, thin content |
| **moneysmart.gov.au** | Government authority | Highest trust, official calculators | Generic, not specialist |
| **industrysuper.com** | Super fund | Excellent super calculators, updated content | Super-focused, not pay-focused |
| **seek.com.au** | Job platform | Huge traffic, salary tools | Pay calculator is secondary feature |
| **talent.com** | Global salary site | Good coverage | Not Australia-specialist |
| **salaryaftertax.com** | Multi-country | Good for comparisons | Generic, not deep on AU specifics |
| **hrblock.com.au** | Tax agent | Authoritative tax content | Commercial focus, upsells |
| **superguide.com.au** | Super education | Comprehensive guides | Primarily super, not pay |

### Content Gaps in Competitor Landscape
1. **No single site covers ALL deductions** — most focus on either tax OR super, not both together
2. **Award rate calculators are missing** — no competitor integrates FWC award rates with a pay calculator
3. **HECS/HELP integration is poor** — most calculators ignore HELP repayments or add them as afterthought
4. **State payroll tax comparison is absent** — no clear state-by-state comparison tool for employers
5. **Salary sacrifice visualization is weak** — most just explain the concept, don't calculate the actual savings
6. **Historical FY comparison** — very few show how your pay changes across financial years
7. **Contractor vs Employee comparison** — biggest gap: no calculator shows the real difference in take-home

## 4. Seasonal Search Patterns

| Period | Event | Search Spike |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| July 1-14 | New financial year begins | "new tax rates" "pay calculator 2025-26" |
| July-October | Tax return season | "how much tax do I pay" "tax calculator" |
| October 31 | Tax return deadline | "tax return" spike |
| January | Back-to-work | "salary calculator" "pay rates" |
| July 1 | Award rate changes | "new minimum wage" "award rates 2025" |
| April-June | EOFY planning | "salary sacrifice" "super contribution" |

### Strategic Implication
**Launch timing**: Ideally publish core content before July 1 (new FY) to capture the massive seasonal spike. Second-best window: January return-to-work season.
