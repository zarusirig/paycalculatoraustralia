# What to build next: research, 23 Oct 2026

Sources: GSC API (domain property, 23 Sep to 20 Oct 2026, 96,594 query+page rows), DataForSEO Google Ads search volume (Australia, Sep 2026, cost $0.09) and DataForSEO live SERPs (Australia). Retrieved 23 Oct 2026. Scripts and raw data were in the session scratchpad, not kept.

## Finding 1: employer pages are nearly done

40 employer pages exist. Every big name with volume is covered (woolworths 1,600/mo, coles 1,000, bunnings 720, hungry jacks 390, aldi 320). The next employers are small: grill'd 140, 7-eleven 90, jb hi fi 90, uniqlo 70, ikea 70, pizza hut 70, myer 50, mecca 50. Everything else is 40 or less. More employer pages now give little.

## Finding 3: nurse pay by state ranks badly

Searches/mo: nurse salary qld 2,900, qld health pay rates 1,900, nurse salary nsw 1,900, nurse salary victoria 880, nsw health pay rates 720, nurse salary wa 170. Plus registered nurse salary 2,400, enrolled nurse 1,600, midwife 2,400.

Our /healthcare-worker-pay/{state}/ pages sit at position 10 to 18 (nsw 11.5, qld 9.9, vic 15.0). Only WA works (3.1% CTR, pos 7.6). SERP for "nurse salary qld": a 2022 Mater PDF, QIRC PDF, ABC news on the QLD nurses deal, fairworkmate. Winnable with a nurse-first page per state.

## Finding 4: health job pages exist but rank low

High volume: pharmacist salary 4,400, radiographer 3,600, physiotherapist 3,600, psychologist 2,900. Pages exist under /job-pay-rates/. Positions: radiographer 9.0, pharmacist 9.7, physio 16.7. SERP is Indeed, Glassdoor, jobsandskills.gov.au. This is an upgrade job, not new pages. Lower priority: Indeed/Glassdoor are strong.

## Other notes

- /public-service-pay-scales/aps/el2/ gets 39 impr while "el2 salary" has 590/mo. The hub ranks instead. Check its internal links and title.
- APS 1, 2, 7 and SES band 1 (390/mo) have no page. Small.
- Junior minimum wage queries: 33k impr at 0.41% CTR. SERP answers them. Leave.

## Recommended build order

1. VPS level pages vps-2 to vps-7 (6 pages), same template as APS levels.
2. QLD AO2 to AO8 and SA ASO2 to ASO8 level pages (14 pages).
3. Nurse pay page per state (start QLD, NSW, VIC), with grade-by-grade tables from the current agreements.
4. Then upgrade the health job pages.
