### Out of Scope — Do Not Cover
These topics are **outside our border** — never write about:

- ❌ Investment advice / financial planning
- ❌ Self-managed super fund (SMSF) administration
- ❌ Property/shares/crypto tax
- ❌ Business tax (company tax rates)
- ❌ International tax / double tax agreements
- ❌ Immigration / visa work rights
- ❌ Accounting software reviews
- ❌ Bookkeeping practices
- ❌ Insurance products (life, income protection)
- ❌ Mortgage / loan calculators (despite keyword data showing volume)

### Border Enforcement Rules
1. **Every page must link back to a calculator** — if content can't organically link to one of our calculators, it's outside the border
2. **Maximum 3 degrees from central entity** — "Australian Pay Calculation" → Income Tax → Tax Brackets (2 degrees). "Australian Pay Calculation" → Investment Tax Strategies (3+ degrees) = out of scope
3. **Real money test**: If the reader can't use the information to check their next payslip, it's probably outside the border

# Part 2: Audience Journey Mapping (Skill 23)

## Audience Segments

### Segment A: Employee (Novice)
**Profile**: First job, new graduate, new to Australia, or just never understood their pay
**Questions**: "How much tax do I pay?", "What is super?", "Why is my pay less than my salary?"
**Emotional state**: Confused, slightly anxious
**Content needs**: Simple explanations, visual breakdowns, real dollar examples
**Entry points**: Homepage calculator, "Understanding Your Payslip" guide
**Journey**:
```
Homepage calculator → Enter salary → See breakdown → "What is Medicare levy?" 
→ Medicare guide → "What about HECS?" → HECS calculator → Bookmark site
```
**Tone**: Warm, reassuring. "Don't worry — here's exactly how it works."
**CTA path**: Use calculator → Bookmark → Email signup ("Get FY updates")

### Segment B: Employee (Experienced)
**Profile**: 3-10 years working, understands basics, wants specific answers
**Questions**: "Tax on $95,000?", "Should I salary sacrifice?", "How much is my pay rise worth?"
**Emotional state**: Pragmatic, time-poor
**Content needs**: Quick answers, comparison tools, "what-if" scenarios
**Entry points**: Specific calculators (salary sacrifice, pay rise), tax brackets page
**Journey**:
```
"Tax on $95,000" → Tax brackets page → Income tax calculator → Salary sacrifice calculator 
→ "How much will I save?" → Decision
```
**Tone**: Efficient, direct. "Here's what you need to know."
**CTA path**: Use calculator → Compare scenarios → Share with colleague

### Segment C: Employer / HR
**Profile**: Small business owner, HR manager, payroll officer
**Questions**: "How much super do I pay?", "Total cost of an employee?", "PAYG tables?"
**Emotional state**: Compliance-focused, wants certainty
**Content needs**: Exact rates, compliance tables, employer-perspective breakdowns
**Entry points**: Employer cost calculator, PAYG tables, super guide
**Journey**:
```
"Employer super contribution" → Super calculator → "What about payroll tax?" 
→ State page → Employer cost calculator → PAYG tables → Bookmark
```
**Tone**: Professional, compliance-aware. "Your obligations under the SG Act."
**CTA path**: Use calculator → Download tables → Email signup ("Rate change alerts")

### Segment D: HECS Graduate
**Profile**: Recent graduate or ongoing HECS debtor wondering about repayment impact
**Questions**: "When do HECS repayments start?", "How much is my repayment?"
**Emotional state**: Anxious about debt, wants clarity
**Content needs**: New marginal system explained, real impact on take-home pay
**Entry points**: HECS calculator, HECS guide
**Journey**:
```
"HECS repayment threshold" → HECS guide → HECS calculator → Take-home pay calculator 
→ "How does salary sacrifice interact?" → Salary sacrifice calculator
```
**Tone**: Empathetic, explanatory. "The new system means most people pay less."
**CTA path**: Use HECS calculator → Check take-home pay → Email signup

### Segment E: Casual/Part-Time Worker
**Profile**: Hospitality, retail, gig worker — paid hourly with variable hours
**Questions**: "Hourly to annual?", "Casual loading?", "Penalty rates?"
**Emotional state**: Wants to verify they're being paid correctly
**Content needs**: Hourly/weekly converters, award rate tables, penalty rate info
**Entry points**: Hourly calculator, weekly calculator, award rates guide
**Journey**:
```
"Hourly pay calculator" → Enter rate → Convert to weekly/annual 
→ "Am I being paid correctly?" → Award rates guide → Fair Work link
```
**Tone**: Practical, empowering. "Check your pay is right."
**CTA path**: Use converter → Check award rate → Bookmark
