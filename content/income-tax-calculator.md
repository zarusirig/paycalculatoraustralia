## Australian Tax Brackets 2025-26

Income tax in Australia is progressive — each portion of your income is taxed at a different rate. Here are the current brackets:

| Taxable Income | Tax Rate | Base Tax |
|---------------|----------|----------|
| $0 – $18,200 | 0% | $0 |
| $18,201 – $45,000 | 16% | — |
| $45,001 – $135,000 | 30% | $4,288 |
| $135,001 – $190,000 | 37% | $31,288 |
| $190,001+ | 45% | $51,638 |

You only pay the higher rate on income within that bracket. On $80,000, your effective tax rate is 18.49% — not the 30% marginal rate that applies to your top bracket.

## How Marginal Tax Works

When your income crosses into a higher bracket, only the portion above the threshold is taxed at the higher rate. Here's a worked example on **$90,000**:

| Bracket | Income | Rate | Tax |
|---------|--------|------|-----|
| $0 – $18,200 | $18,200 | 0% | $0 |
| $18,201 – $45,000 | $26,800 | 16% | $4,288 |
| $45,001 – $90,000 | $45,000 | 30% | $13,500 |
| **Total** | **$90,000** | — | **$17,788** |

Your effective rate: 19.76%. Your marginal rate: 30%.

## Tax Offsets That Reduce Your Bill

### Low Income Tax Offset (LITO)

If you earn under $66,667, the LITO automatically reduces your tax by up to $700:

- Income up to $37,500: full $700 offset
- $37,501 – $45,000: reduces at 5c per dollar over $37,500
- $45,001 – $66,667: reduces at 1.5c per dollar over $45,000

Combined with the tax-free threshold, the LITO means you can earn up to $22,575 before paying any net income tax.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How much tax do I pay on $80,000?
On $80,000, you pay $14,788 in income tax for FY2025-26. After the 2% Medicare levy ($1,600), your total tax deductions are $16,388. Your take-home pay is $63,612 per year — $1,223.31 per week.

### Do I pay my marginal rate on my entire salary?
No. Australia uses a progressive system. You only pay the marginal rate on the portion of income within that bracket. On $80,000, your marginal rate is 30%, but your effective rate is only 18.49%.

### What is the tax-free threshold?
The tax-free threshold is $18,200. You pay no income tax on the first $18,200 you earn. With LITO, the effective threshold is $22,575.

### How is income tax collected?
Your employer deducts income tax from each pay through the PAYG (Pay As You Go) withholding system. The amount withheld is based on the [ATO's tax tables](/payg-withholding-tables/). You reconcile any difference when you lodge your annual tax return.
