## Super Guarantee Rate — 12% from July 2025

The Superannuation Guarantee (SG) rate for FY2025-26 is **12%** of your Ordinary Time Earnings. This is the final step in a series of legislated increases:

| Financial Year | SG Rate |
|---------------|---------|
| 2021-22 | 10% |
| 2022-23 | 10.5% |
| 2023-24 | 11% |
| 2024-25 | 11.5% |
| **2025-26** | **12%** |

The 12% rate is now permanent — no further legislated increases are scheduled.

## What Counts as Ordinary Time Earnings

SG is calculated on your Ordinary Time Earnings (OTE), not your total pay:

**Included in OTE:**
- Base salary and wages
- Commissions
- Shift loadings
- Paid leave (annual, personal, long service)
- Some allowances and bonuses

**Not included in OTE:**
- Overtime payments
- Unused leave on termination
- Expense reimbursements
- Workers compensation payments

## Maximum Super Contribution Base

For FY2025-26, the maximum super contribution base is **$62,500 per quarter** ($250,000 per year). Your employer isn't required to pay SG on earnings above this limit.

At 12%, the maximum quarterly SG contribution is **$7,500** ($30,000 per year).

If you earn above $250,000 and want more super, you can make voluntary contributions — but be aware of the [contribution caps](/superannuation-guide/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is super included in my salary?
Typically, no. Most employment contracts quote your base salary with super paid on top. However, some contracts state a "total package" that includes super. Check your contract — if it says "plus super" or "plus SG," super is additional. If it says "including super," it's already included.

### How much super should I have at 30?
A common benchmark is 1x your annual salary by age 30. On an $80,000 salary, that's an $80,000 super balance. If you're behind, [salary sacrifice](/salary-sacrifice-calculator/) and voluntary contributions can help you catch up.

### Do casual workers get super?
Yes. Since 1 July 2024, all employees — including casuals — are entitled to SG regardless of how much they earn. The previous $450/month threshold has been abolished.

### Can my employer pay super less than 12%?
No. 12% SG is the legal minimum. Employers who don't pay on time face the Super Guarantee Charge (SGC), which includes the unpaid amount, interest, and an administration fee.
