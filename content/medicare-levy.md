# Medicare Levy 2025-26 — Rate, Surcharge, Thresholds & Exemptions

The Medicare levy is a 2% charge on your taxable income that helps fund Australia's public healthcare system. Most Australian resident taxpayers pay it. Here's how it works, who's exempt, and how the surcharge applies if you don't have private health insurance.

---

## Medicare Levy Rate

The standard Medicare levy is **2% of your taxable income**. It's charged on top of your income tax.

| Salary | Medicare Levy (2%) | Per Week |
|--------|-------------------|----------|
| $40,000 | $800 | $15.38 |
| $60,000 | $1,200 | $23.08 |
| $80,000 | $1,600 | $30.77 |
| $100,000 | $2,000 | $38.46 |
| $120,000 | $2,400 | $46.15 |
| $150,000 | $3,000 | $57.69 |

---

## Low-Income Exemptions and Reductions

If your taxable income is below the low-income threshold, you pay a reduced levy or no levy at all:

- **Singles below ~$27,222**: No Medicare levy
- **Singles $27,222–$34,028**: Reduced levy (phased in at 10% of excess)
- **Families below ~$45,924** (+ $4,213 per dependent child): No levy

These thresholds are adjusted annually. The figures above are from FY2024-25 — FY2025-26 thresholds are typically confirmed by the ATO after the end of the income year.

---

## Medicare Levy Surcharge (MLS)

The MLS is an **additional** charge of 1% to 1.5% for higher-income earners who don't have private hospital cover. It's separate from the standard 2% levy.

| Income (Singles) | Income (Families) | MLS Rate |
|-----------------|------------------|----------|
| $93,001 – $108,000 | $186,001 – $216,000 | 1.0% |
| $108,001 – $144,000 | $216,001 – $288,000 | 1.25% |
| $144,001+ | $288,001+ | 1.5% |

On a $150,000 salary without private health insurance, the MLS costs an additional **$1,875** per year (1.25% × $150,000). A basic hospital policy typically costs $1,200–$1,800 per year — often cheaper than the surcharge.

### How to Avoid the MLS

Hold private hospital cover (not just extras cover) with an excess of $750 or less (singles) or $1,500 or less (families). The cover must be compliant and held for the full year.

---

## Who Is Exempt From the Medicare Levy?

You may be exempt or pay a reduced levy if you:
- Earn below the low-income threshold
- Are a non-resident for the full year (non-residents don't pay the levy)
- Hold a specific Medicare exemption category (e.g., some visa holders)
- Are a member of a foreign defence force

You claim these exemptions when lodging your tax return.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Medicare levy included in the tax tables?
No. The 2% Medicare levy is calculated separately from income tax. When you see [tax brackets](/tax-brackets/) on the ATO website, they don't include the levy. Your total effective tax rate is your income tax rate plus 2%.

### Can I opt out of paying the Medicare levy?
Generally, no. If you're an Australian resident for tax purposes, the levy is mandatory. The only exceptions are for certain non-residents, some visa holders, and specific exemption categories.

### Is the Medicare levy the same as the Medicare Levy Surcharge?
No. The Medicare levy (2%) applies to nearly all residents. The Medicare Levy Surcharge (1%–1.5%) is an **additional** charge that only applies to higher earners ($93,001+ singles) who don't have private hospital cover.
