# HECS-HELP Repayment Calculator 2025-26 — New Marginal System

Calculate your HECS-HELP repayment under the new marginal rate system that started in FY2025-26. The threshold has risen to $67,000, and repayments are now calculated only on income above the threshold — not your entire salary.

## What Changed in FY2025-26

The biggest change to HECS in decades. The system moved from **flat-rate** to **marginal-rate** repayments:

| | Old System (Before FY2025-26) | New System (FY2025-26+) |
|---|-------------------------------|------------------------|
| **How it worked** | Flat % on total income | Marginal rate on income above threshold |
| **Threshold** | $54,435 | **$67,000** |
| **Smallest repayment** | 1% of entire income ($544) | 15c per $1 over $67,000 |
| **Impact** | Repayment jumped suddenly above threshold | Gradual increase — no cliff edge |

Under the old system, earning $1 over the threshold triggered a repayment on your entire income. Under the new system, you only pay on the amount above $67,000.

## HECS-HELP Repayment Rates 2025-26

| Repayment Income | Rate | How It's Calculated |
|-----------------|------|-------------------|
| $0 – $67,000 | Nil | No compulsory repayment |
| $67,001 – $125,000 | 15c per $1 over $67,000 | Marginal rate on excess |
| $125,001 – $179,285 | $8,700 + 17c per $1 over $125,000 | Higher marginal on this band |
| $179,286 and over | 10% of total repayment income | Flat rate applies at top |

**Repayment income** includes your taxable income plus net investment losses, reportable fringe benefits, reportable super contributions, and exempt foreign employment income.

## Should You Make Voluntary Repayments?

Voluntary repayments reduce your HECS debt faster, but there's no financial incentive beyond reducing the balance. Since HECS is indexed to CPI (not charged interest), the effective rate is usually lower than a mortgage or personal loan.

**Consider voluntary repayments if:**
- You have no other debts with higher interest rates
- You want to remove the HECS impact from your take-home pay
- You're planning a mortgage application (some lenders factor HECS into servicing)

**Don't prioritise voluntary repayments if:**
- You have credit card debt, car loans, or personal loans
- You'd rather invest the money elsewhere
- Your debt will eventually be written off (currently at age 67 or death)

---

## HECS Indexation

HECS debts are indexed annually on 1 June, based on the Consumer Price Index (CPI). This isn't interest — it's an adjustment to maintain the real value of the debt. The indexation rate varies each year but has historically ranged from 1% to 7%.

Any voluntary repayments made before 1 June reduce the balance that gets indexed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### At what income do HECS repayments start?
Compulsory HECS repayments start when your repayment income reaches $67,000 in FY2025-26 (up from $54,435 in FY2024-25). Below $67,000, you make no compulsory repayment.

### How much is my HECS repayment on $80,000?
On $80,000, your compulsory HECS repayment is $1,950 per year ($37.50 per week). This is calculated at 15 cents per dollar on the $13,000 above the $67,000 threshold.

### Do HECS repayments come out of my pay?
Yes. Your employer deducts HECS repayments through the PAYG system if you've indicated a HELP debt on your Tax File Number declaration. The amount is withheld from each pay cycle, just like income tax.

### Can I reduce my HECS repayment with salary sacrifice?
[Salary sacrificing into super](/salary-sacrifice-calculator/) reduces your taxable income, which may reduce your repayment income and therefore your HECS repayment. However, reportable super contributions are added back to calculate repayment income, so the benefit is limited.
